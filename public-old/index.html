Frequently asked

What does Yugi actually do?

Yugi watches the tasks, messages, and notes you point it at, keeps a running picture of what's outstanding, and tells you in plain language what to do next.

Which tools can Yugi connect to?

Most task managers, calendars, and mail providers. If something isn't supported yet, you can still hand Yugi context by hand.

Is my data kept private?

Your task data is used only to run your account and is never sold. You can disconnect any linked tool or delete your account at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — plans are month to month, and cancelling takes one click from your account settings.

Does Yugi work for teams?

The Team plan shares context and daily briefs across a group, while keeping each person's private tasks private.
